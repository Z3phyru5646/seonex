Easy activity guides for groups

Each guide gives you a clear setup, a suggested time box, and one way to adapt for different people.

Guided neighborhood photo walk

Choose five visual prompts, pair people up, take a slow route, and end with a ten-minute share. This activity works well for new groups because conversation has a natural starting point.

Skill-swap circle

Ask each person to bring a small practical skill. Run short demonstrations, rotate tables, and leave room for questions. Start with our broad activities ideas if you are still choosing a format.
